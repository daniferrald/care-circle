# Message Examples

Reference examples only, used to calibrate tone and the disclosure boundary (see help_policy.md, sections 3 and 5). Not inserted verbatim.

## 1. Sympathy + suggestion — patient perspective, short stay
"I'm sorry to hear you're at the ER, and I hope the fracture turns out to be straightforward. Based on what you've shared, it sounds like you might be off your feet for a day or two. Would it help to ask a couple of people in your Care Circle for meals and groceries?"

## 2. Sympathy + suggestion — caregiver perspective, unclear stay length
"That sounds like a scary morning — I'm glad you got her seen quickly. Since we don't know yet how long the stay will be, want me to check in again once there's a clearer picture, or would it help to ask someone now just in case?"

## 3. Outbound request — no-diagnosis, need-based framing
"Hi Sydney — I'm dealing with a hospital stay this week and could use a hand with meals if you're able. No pressure at all if the timing doesn't work. [Care Circle sign-up / respond link]"

## 4. Outbound request — combined help types, one message, sent to a single-type recipient
This is the exact message from case EVAL-01, drafted once and sent unchanged to both Sydney Adamu (opted into Meals only) and Tina Marrero (opted into Groceries only) — neither is expected to cover both:
"Hi Tina — I'm in the hospital for a few days and could use help with two things: meals and groceries. Either one (or both, if you're able) would be a huge help — no need to cover everything on the list. Thank you for being part of my Care Circle. [Care Circle sign-up / respond link]"

## 5. No-match acknowledgment
"I don't see anyone in your Care Circle currently set up for dog walking on a weekday. Would you like me to ask an existing member anyway, even though it's outside what they've signed up for?"

## 6. Outbound request — "ask anyway" to a non-matched recipient
"Hi Donna — I know dog walking isn't really what you signed up for in my Care Circle, but I'm in a bit of a bind this week and wondered if you might be able to help just this once. No worries at all if it's not a good fit — totally fine to say no. [Care Circle sign-up / respond link]"
